**Regulations for the Master Craftsman's Examination in Parts I and II in the Agricultural and Construction Machinery Mechatronics Trade**

---

## Part I of the Master Craftsman Examination (Practical Skills)

Part I comprises exactly **three examination components**:

### 1. Master craftsman examination project (§ 5 MPVO)
- Creation of a complex project plan, installation, configuration or diagnostics on an agricultural or construction machine.
- **Weighting:** Implementation work (50%), planning documents (25%), documentation & control (25%).
- **System selection:** For the customer order, 2 systems must be selected from the 7 legally defined system categories (1–4: electrohydraulics, electropneumatics, mechanics, mechatronics; 5–7: drive, networking, autonomy).

### 2nd Expert Discussion (§ 6 MPVO)
- Max. 30-minute technical discussion based on the completed master craftsman examination project (customer consultation, justification of procedure, error analysis).

### 3. Situational task (§ 7 MPVO)
- Practical diagnostic and repair task on networked mechatronic systems (duration: approx. 6 to 8 hours).

---

## Part II of the Master Craftsman Examination (Specialized Theory)

Part II is examined in **three written examination areas**:

### 1. Area of Action 1
Analyze customer requirements, develop and offer solutions (case study / specialist theory).

### 2. Area of Action 2
Providing, monitoring, and handing over the services of a company.

### 3. Area of Action 3
Managing and organizing a business in the agricultural and construction machinery mechatronics trade (commercial/legal).
