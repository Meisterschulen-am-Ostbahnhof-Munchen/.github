**Framework curriculum for the master craftsman's examination in the agricultural and construction machinery mechatronics trade**

---

## Part I – Practical Training (380 teaching units)

### Field of activity 1: Extensions to agricultural and construction machinery (160 teaching units)
- **LE 1.1 (20 UE):** Order acceptance, analysis of customer requirements & workshop organization.
- **LE 1.2 (60 UE):** Planning & development of mechatronic solutions (design, circuit diagrams).
- **LE 1.3 (40 UE):** Practical installation, wiring & assembly of mechatronic accessories.
- **LE 1.4 (40 UE):** Parameter adjustment, calibration, commissioning of networked systems.

### Field of Action 2: Diagnosis and Repair (220 teaching units)
- **LE 2.1 (40 UE):** Structured troubleshooting & repair planning for machines.
- **LE 2.2 (40 UE):** Fault diagnosis of vehicle & machine functions (sensors, actuators, bus).
- **LE 2.3 (40 UE):** Fault diagnosis on drive systems (diesel engines, Stage V, HV, transmissions).
- **LE 2.4 (40 UE):** Fault diagnosis on hydraulic, electro-hydraulic & pneumatic systems.
- **LE 2.5 (40 UE):** Fault diagnosis of transmission systems, telemetry, GNSS & customer IT.
- **LE 2.6 (20 UE):** Fault diagnosis on autonomous & automated systems.

---

## Part II – Subject-Specific Theory (810 teaching hours)

### Area of activity 1: Analyzing requirements, developing and offering solutions (380 hours)
- **LE 1.1 (160 hours):** Mechanical and systems engineering as well as technical fundamentals.
- **LE 1.2 (40 hours):** Maintenance technology, servicing & repair processes.
- **LE 1.3 (100 hours):** Diagnostic technology & troubleshooting.
- **LE 1.4 (40 hours):** Creating plans, sketches & technical drawings.
- **LE 1.5 (40 hours):** Customer requirements & order-specific framework conditions.

### Area of activity 2: Providing, controlling & handing over the services of a company (260 hours)
- **LE 2.1 (80 hours):** Preparation & execution of work processes in customer service.
- **LE 2.2 (80 hours):** Quality control, performance monitoring & fault prevention.
- **LE 2.3 (60 hours):** Invoicing, post-calculation & handover to the customer.
- **LE 2.4 (40 hours):** Application of workshop identification systems.
